Ask a question
How much does a share cost?

Shares are priced per horse and include purchase, training and vet fees for the season. We send the full breakdown when you enquire, with no hidden extras.

Do I need any racing experience?

None at all. Most of our members are new to ownership. We explain everything in plain language and keep you updated from the stables to race day.

Can I visit the horses?

Yes. Members are invited to stable visits, morning gallops and race days, with owners' badges where available.

What happens with prize money?

Prize money is shared between members in proportion to their share, after standard racing deductions, and reported in every statement.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Each syndicate runs for a set term, usually one or two seasons. You will know the end date and the exit options before you join.
