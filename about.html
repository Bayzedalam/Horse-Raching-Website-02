A modern homefor racing owners.

Meridian started with a simple belief: the best seat in racing should not be reserved for the few.

Our story

Born at the rail.

Meridian was founded by lifelong racing fans who spent years watching from the public stands, wishing they could be part of the story behind the horse.

Traditional ownership felt closed: expensive, complicated and full of jargon. So we set out to build a syndicate that feels modern, honest and genuinely welcoming.

We partner with licensed trainers, select every horse with care, and keep each syndicate small, so every member knows the horse, the team and the plan.

Whether you dream of a winner's photo or simply love early mornings on the gallops, there is a place for you here.

What we stand for

Our mission, in three lines.

Everything we do is measured against these three promises to our members.

01 · Access

Open the gate

Make thoroughbred ownership affordable and approachable for everyday fans.

02 · Honesty

No small print

Every cost, every fee and every decision shared openly with members.

03 · Experience

Feel it all

Stable visits, race days and real updates, so you live every stride.
